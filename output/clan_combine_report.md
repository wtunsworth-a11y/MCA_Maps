# Clans tested separately and combined

Each clan walked by more than one custodian, assessed both as separate surveys and as one boundary.

**Nothing here is applied automatically.** Whether several surveys describe one boundary is a question about the clan, not the geometry. This is the evidence for asking.

`duplication_pct` is the share of walked distance running within 20 m of another custodian's track. High means they re-walked the same line — independent accounts, so combining is meaningless. Low means they walked different stretches, which is what one boundary split between walkers looks like.
