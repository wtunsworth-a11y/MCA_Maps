# Sacred sites

**Locations are not shared.** At the community's decision, sacred sites are reported by area and share of clan land only. This report carries no coordinates, no map and no site geometry, and they are withheld from every project file and exported layer.

3 site(s) across 2 mapped unit(s) and 3 walk(s).

Every area is an estimate: none of these walks closes into a ring. **Bridged** joins the open ends with straight lines (comparable with the clan boundary figures); **hull** is the convex hull of the walk, an upper bound. The true area lies between them.
