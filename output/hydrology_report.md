# Modelled drainage

Derived from the Copernicus 30 m DEM — depressions filled, D8 flow routing, channels extracted above 1,000 upstream cells (~0.90 km² of drainage).

**This is modelled drainage, not surveyed rivers.** It shows where water would run given the terrain. Treat it as a prompt for the field team, never as evidence a boundary is right or wrong.

- **Channel segments:** 2,178
- **Total channel length:** 3,527 km
- **Boundary walked along drainage** (within 50 m): **18.8%**
