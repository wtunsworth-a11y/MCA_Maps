# Must-haves for a clan map

A running log, not a specification. Each line is something we agreed
while looking at a real clan, and it has to be true of every clan map
after that. Add to it when we agree something new. If a map goes
backwards on one of these, say so rather than letting it pass.

Order is the order we agreed them.

---

1. **A map every time.** Not a description of a map.
   *Dusi, 11 Oct.*

2. **One clan at a time**, and when we are discussing chord 3, do not
   write about chords 2 and 4.
   *Riribudeh, 11 Oct.*

3. **All the data, not what the pipeline chose to use.** Walked line the
   polygon leaves out is drawn and its length given. Mungaikol's 5.4 km
   was invisible because the map was built from the survivors.
   *Mungaikol, 11 Oct.*

4. **Nothing withheld from the review.** The sacred-site rule governs
   what is shared, not what the people deciding may see. Withholding
   happens once, on the way out to a document.
   *Sukandi and Mungaikol, 11 Oct.*

5. **Labels off the lines and off the land.** A marker on a chord hides
   the chord; a legend on the polygon hides the boundary. Both sit
   clear, with a leader back.
   *Dusi and Mungaikol, 11 Oct.*

6. **Four categories, kept apart:**
   - walked;
   - recorded walks chained into one line — the ground *is* walked, this
     asserts nothing;
   - inferred where nobody walked — a river, a ridge, a line across;
     this is what the clan is asked to confirm;
   - still to walk.
   Chaining is not inference. Calling it so would have sent Gumuyude's
   clan to confirm 2 km of their own steward's footsteps.
   *Gumuyude, 11 Oct.*

7. **The area is mapping progress, not ownership.** It stays in the
   legend as "N ha enclosed by the mapping so far", with the footnote
   that boundaries not yet confirmed will move and overlaps are not yet
   measured. It does not go in the title.
   *Manuvoora, 11 Oct.*

8. **Every recording is drawn as a line.** A short recording is still a
   steward walking. Drawing it as a dot made 452 m of Dusi's walk at
   garegummara hill look like bare ground.
   *Dusi, 11 Oct.*

9. **A recording with no description is not "unexplained".** The walking
   is the record; the name is the extra to go back and ask for.
   *Manuvoora, 11 Oct.*

10. **Read every field.** Stewards type into whichever box the app shows
    them — `name`, `desc`, `cmt`, `Name`, `description`. Reading one of
    them is why Sukandi's sacred sites are protected and Mungaikol's is
    carrying an outer boundary.
    *Mungaikol, 11 Oct.*

11. **Never match by a typed name.** Clan names are spelled eight ways,
    stewards five. `manuvuoora` lost Granville Nepo's entire walk. Match
    by the source file, or by the folder the data was uploaded in.
    *Manuvoora, 11 Oct.*

12. **Do not state a finding before rendering it.** "Manuvoora stands",
    "chord 2 is cosmetic", "519 ha mostly inferred", "16 of 21 places
    outside the boundary" — all computed, narrated, and wrong, and all
    would have been caught by drawing them first.
    *11 Oct, after six of them.*

13. **A length threshold is not a classifier.** 452 m of boundary
    walking was called a point because it was under 500 m. Ask what the
    shape says — a recording that returns near its own start is a place,
    one that goes somewhere is a stretch.
    *Dusi, 11 Oct.*

14. **Chained or inferred is decided at 10 m, against the smoothed
    layer.** Not 30 m, which measures how near the walk passes rather
    than whether the ground was walked — Riribudeh's 48 m straight join
    is 100% at 30 m and nobody walked it. And not 5 m, which is tighter
    than the stewards' own agreement with each other: a pair walking one
    boundary differ by median 3 m and 12 m at the 90th percentile, so
    5 m called Solomon Makanisa's walk "not walked" on Sahirut because
    Lenard Urami's line was the reference. Judge against the smoothed
    layer the segment came from, not the raw tracks, or the comparison
    measures the smoothing.
    *Riribudeh and Sahirut, 11 Oct.*

15. **Do not move to the next clan until told.** One clan at a time
    means waiting, not queueing. Running ahead through Majanko and Murai
    while Sahirut was still open put three maps on the table at once and
    made it impossible to review any of them.
    *Sahirut, 11 Oct.*
